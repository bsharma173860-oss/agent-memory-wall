# Summary: KV-cache energy study, analysis of the recorded results

Generated by `analyze.py` from all-results.zip and extra-results.zip. GPU board energy only (NVML). Details: tables.md (every cell, both rounds), analysis.md (fairness, models, CPU bound), figures/. Hypotheses H11-H16 and H17/H18 have no data in these two zips and are not judged here.

## Verdicts

- **H1 (engines within 3%): partly supported.** Default vs default, SGLang/vLLM J per output token differ by at most +2.3% (chat 8/s); tuned vs tuned by up to -5.7% (rag 8/s) — outside 3%, because SGLang's tuned flags cut RAG 8/s to 0.595 J/token (TTFT p90 2.3 s vs 12.6 s default) while vLLM tuning changes little (mean |difference| 0.29% over the 12 cells, largest 1.5% at RAG 8/s, -1.5%; the plan says 0.1%). Workload range 12.3x (> 5x: supported).

- **H2 (miss penalty grows with load and length): supported (one round only).** Miss/hit J per output token at 1 user: 1.01x/1.01x (2k, vLLM/SGLang), 1.46x/1.48x (16k) — all <= 1.5x; at 64 users 3.0x/3.0x (2k) and 10.1x/10.6x (16k, >= 5x). The 8k point is part 1 and not in these zips. E2 has no second round.

- **H3 (offload >= 20% less energy and >= 2x lower resume TTFT p90 at 48 sessions): not supported.** SGLang 80 GB: 958 vs 1058 J/turn (-9.4%), hit 12.0%, resume TTFT p90 56.7 vs 70.9 s (1.25x). vLLM 70 GiB: 1035 vs 1049 (-1.3%), hit 1.7%. vLLM at 80 GB never ran (/dev/shm). 'No pressure (16 sessions) within noise' is **not supported** either: offload is -5.2% (SGLang) and -6.2% (vLLM) per turn at 16 sessions/5 s with ~89% vs ~85% hit, while rounds repeat within ~1%. 'Benefit shrinks at 96': yes (-2.3% SGLang 80 GB).

- **H4 (offload benefit larger at 30 s pauses): not supported overall (mixed).** Change in J/turn vs GPU-only, 48 sessions, 5 s -> 30 s: SGLang 80 GB -9.4% -> -9.2% (same); SGLang 160 GB -57.5% -> -41.3% (smaller); vLLM 70 GiB -1.3% -> -6.0% (larger, from a 1.7% -> 7.9% hit).

- **H5 (offload's own CPU + host-memory energy < 10% of the GPU energy saved): supported where offload saves real energy, as a bound only; not supported where it saves little.** SGLang 160 GB, 48/5 s (inferred alignment): engine CPU 0.88 vs 1.91 s/turn for GPU-only, i.e. *less* per turn (SGLang's scheduler keeps ~1 core busy whatever it does, so per-turn CPU follows wall time per turn, and offload completes more turns); the bound is then set by the assumed host-memory traffic, 3.1 J/turn at 1 nJ/byte, against 608 J of GPU energy saved (0.5%; charging the offload engine's entire CPU time at 10 W/core: 1.5%). SGLang 80 GB, 48/5 s: 2.9% of a 100 J saving. vLLM 70 GiB, 48/5 s: GPU saving only 13.8 J/turn, ratio 25%; at 96/5 s 212%. At 16 sessions the ratio is 12-14% only because of the assumed memory term. Host CPU and DRAM were not metered (no RAPL); a lower J/byte would move every ratio down.

- **H6 (gate: >= 30% hit and >= 25% less energy at 48 sessions, both pauses): supported at 5 s, not at 30 s.** gate 16x4 48/5s 614 vs 1049 J (-41%), hit 65%; gate 16x8 48/5s 558 vs 1049 J (-47%), hit 78%; gate 16x4 48/30s 1093 vs 1034 J (+6%), hit 60%; gate 16x8 48/30s 1118 vs 1034 J (+8%), hit 70%. At 96/5 s: 603/591 vs 1026 J. **Latency cost:** sessions never served in a 150 s step: 6-32 of 48 and 53-80 of 96; no session was ever re-admitted. Wait p90 over all turns, q=4: 82 s (48/5 s), 87 s (96/5 s); first-turn wait p90 127-138 s at 48 sessions; the recorded resume-turn gate wait is 0 s in every step. p90 latency to first token (wait + TTFT, served turns) at 48/5 s: 84 s (q=4) and 17 s (q=8) vs 53 s GPU-only; with never-served sessions counted (censored) 118 / 143 s. Part of the per-turn saving comes from serving a warm subset.

- **H7 (LFU >= 20% hit at 48; SLRU < 5%; 16-session energy within 5%): LFU not supported, SLRU supported.** 48/5 s hit: LFU 0.21%, SLRU 0.21%, LRU 0.12%; J/turn 1059.8/1059.7 vs 1057.8. 16/5 s: LFU -3.7%, SLRU -2.3%; 16/30 s +1.2% / +1.3% (within 5%).

- **H8 (cliff model A at N*~23 / W/C 1, or B at N*~38 / W/C 1.58): neither fits both pauses.** Step hit, 5 s: 16: 84.77%, 24: 20.14%, 32: 0.02%, 40: 0.02%, 48: 0.04%; 30 s: 16: 67.14%, 24: 54.83%, 32: 29.40%, 40: 8.84%, 48: 0.05%. Resumed-turn hit falls to half its 16-session level at W/C ~1.18 (5 s) and ~1.49 (30 s); the last W/C with hits > 2% is 1.22 (5 s) and 1.86 (30 s). Both step-function models miss the partial-hit points by tens of percentage points (models.md (d)). Model A is close at 5 s pauses, and the 30 s collapse lies beyond model B's 1.58.

- **H9 (160 GB: >= 50% hit and >= 20% less energy at 48, both pauses; < 10% benefit at 96): 48 supported, 96 not.** 48/5 s: hit 75.5%, 449.7 J (-57%), 213 turns vs 116; 48/30 s: hit 64.3%, -41%. 96 sessions: -16% (5 s) and -16% (30 s), larger than the < 10% predicted.

- **H10 (restore <= 1/10 of recompute; GPU hit <= 1/20; control = recompute within 10%): supported.** Recompute/restore = 110x / 161x / 221x at nominal 4k/8k/16k (actual ~5.0k/10.0k/20.3k tokens). GPU hit <= 0.00026 J/token. Control after flush vs recompute energy: -0.3% to +3.5%.

- **Seeds replicate:** s1/s2 at 16/5 s hit 87.2%/87.5%, 48/5 s cache vs no cache -1.2% J/turn.

- **Thrashing = no cache (theory sec. 3): confirmed.** GPU-only vs no cache at 48/96 sessions: vLLM -1.51% to -0.54%, SGLang -0.04% to +0.07% J/turn.

## Where the data disagree with, or are missing from, the plan doc

1. The plan's refit says f = 0.76 predicts the other offload cells 'all within 0.5%', but it lists only three easy cells. The same model gives 534 vs 614 J (-12.9%) for 160 GB at 48 sessions, 30 s pause, which is not mentioned there.

2. H10's prompt lengths are labeled ~4k/8k/16k tokens; the measured prompts are 4,962/10,066/20,336 tokens (script `--lengths` is approximate). The plan's 'FLOP model 1.19x' uses the nominal lengths; at the actual lengths the model gives 1.24x vs 1.26x measured, so most of the 'extra' growth disappears.

3. H3's no-pressure clause: offload is ~5-6% cheaper per turn at 16 sessions on both engines, reproducibly (not 'within noise'); the plan never reports the 16-session offload cells.

4. Model (A) of H8 predicts no hits at 16 sessions/5 s (W/C 1.06 > 1), but 97% of resumed turns hit there; W from final prompt sizes overstates the working set during the step, as the plan notes. Model B predicts full hits at 24 and 32 sessions/5 s (W/C 1.22, 1.55 <= ~1.55), where 29% and 0% of resumed turns hit. The plan's reading (collapse near 1.2 at 5 s, 1.9-2.2 at 30 s) matches the data; neither model fits.

5. Gate fairness, recomputed here: never served 6-80 sessions per step across all gate cells (plan: 6-32 of 48); first-turn wait p90 up to 144 s and max 147 s; recorded `gate_wait_p90` is 0.0 s in every step. Up to 31 sessions per step had given back their slot and were (or would have been) waiting again at the end, a second censored wait that is not recorded anywhere. The gate's p90 latency to first token at 48/5 s with q=4 (84 s) is worse than GPU-only's (53 s), even before counting the sessions it never serves.

6. E2 and the seeds ran one round each, while the plan's E2 row and the reviewer table imply two-round repeatability; seed differences are between different sessions, not round noise.

7. SGLang's GPU KV capacity is 251,423 tokens, 2.7% more than vLLM's 244,928 that the plan uses for both engines; the cliff table uses each engine's own value.

8. H5 cannot be judged from CPU energy directly (no RAPL): the bound uses engine CPU time at 10 W/core and an assumed 1 nJ/byte for host memory. The 160 GB run's CPU alignment is inferred from engine stops (no mtime log for it), validated on the 70 GiB and main runs (CPU-seconds within 3%). Two main-run vLLM GPU-only steps (round 1, 16/30 s and 48/5 s) started before the CPU logger and one (16/5 s) was saved before it; the gate, LFU/SLRU, cliff and seed runs have no CPU alignment.

9. HotCarbon's 'throughput collapse at >= 64 GiB' is not seen for vLLM at 70 GiB: turns 114 vs 113 (48/5 s), 161 vs 161 (96/5 s). No failed turns in any run.

10. Results for H11-H18 quoted in the plan (controller, real trajectories, BFCL, TTL, MoE, sizing, write_back) are not in these zips and are unverified here.
